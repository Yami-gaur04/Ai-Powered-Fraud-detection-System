How Scoring Works

The fraud score is a weighted average of 4 risk factors:

  • Amount — vs high limit & user average
  • Velocity — tx count in last hour
  • Location — new vs known locations
  • Device — new vs known devices

Score range: 0 (safe) → 1 (high risk)
